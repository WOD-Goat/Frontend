## HomePage V2 — Remaining items to address

1. Multi-workout day strip dot priority
   When a day has more than one workout (e.g. a personal + a group workout), the week strip dot
   currently reflects whichever workout appears first in the array. It should instead show the
   worst-case status so the user is never misled by a green dot when a missed workout is hiding
   behind it. Priority order: missed > upcoming > completed > empty.

2. Pull-to-refresh
   The week loads once on mount with no way to refresh. If a coach assigns a workout mid-day, or
   the user completes a workout in another tab/device, the home screen stays stale until the app
   restarts. Add pull-to-refresh (ScrollView refreshControl) that re-calls loadWeek().

4. Expired subscription — workout access gating
   Users whose subscription has expired should not be able to open or interact with workouts.
   Currently the home screen shows all week workouts regardless of subscription status. Need to
   check the user's subscription state (via useFeatureGuard or a dedicated check) and either:
   - Block the day card tap and show an upsell prompt, or
   - Show a locked overlay on the card with a "Renew plan" CTA.
   Also applies to the MakeupCallout — tapping a missed workout should not navigate to the
   workout detail if the subscription is expired.

3. Today goes stale across midnight
   `today` is memoized with empty deps — frozen at the moment the component first mounts. A user
   who keeps the app open past midnight will still see the previous day highlighted as today and
   isToday will be wrong. Fix by deriving today inside a useEffect that recalculates at midnight
   (e.g. setTimeout to next midnight) or by refreshing on AppState "active" transition.
